# Speaking analysis checklist

Write down concrete evidence (a short quote, how often it appears), not impressions.

## Spoken style
- How they open: straight into the headline, a scene-setter, a question back?
- Layering: headline first then the why, or build-up to the point?
- Sentence rhythm: short chained clauses ("so ... and ..."), long winding ones, or a mix?
- Where the reason goes: before the claim or trailed after it?
- Connectors and fillers ("so", "I mean", "you know", "basically", "whatever else is there", "etc etc"). Note which also appear in their writing.
- How they address the listener ("bro", "man", first names, nothing).
- Precision: do they gesture ("some MCP somewhere") or specify exactly?
- Honesty markers: how they admit limits or bugs ("it's vibe coded", "not public, too buggy").
- Code-switching: how the non-technical explanation differs from the technical one.
- How they react when interrupted or corrected (useful for the "how to behave" part of the profile).
- Transcription artifacts to ignore: misheard words, broken spellings of names, dropped syllables.
